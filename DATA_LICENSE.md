Weather data by [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/), obtained through its Historical Weather API using the **ERA5** model. Underlying reanalysis source: [Copernicus Climate Change Service](https://climate.copernicus.eu/). Location data by [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/), accessed through Open-Meteo's Geocoding API.

Raw responses retain their original values; the curated CSV joins city information, renames fields with explicit units, and derives month, season, diurnal temperature range, and a wet-day indicator. Summary tables and charts aggregate these values. No endorsement by Open-Meteo, GeoNames, or Copernicus is implied.
